[Original prompt]
A player misses a shot, the cue ball gently rolling to a stop near a group of balls.

Relevant physical states:

* The player, cue stick, cue ball, and group of balls must remain present and visually consistent during the sequence.
* The cue ball should begin moving only after being struck by the cue stick.
* The cue ball's position should change continuously as it rolls across the table.
* The cue ball should not successfully enter a pocket or complete the intended shot.
* The cue ball's speed should gradually decrease as it rolls.
* The group of balls should remain spatially consistent unless physically contacted.
* The cue ball should approach the group of balls without requiring a strong collision.
* The cue ball should eventually come to rest near the group of balls.

Physical event order:

1. The player strikes the cue ball with the cue stick.
2. The cue ball begins rolling across the table.
3. The attempted shot is missed, and the cue ball continues moving without achieving the intended result.
4. The cue ball approaches the group of balls while gradually slowing down.
5. The cue ball loses its remaining motion.
6. The cue ball comes to a stop near the group of balls.

Cause-effect constraints:

* The cue ball must exist and be struck by the cue stick before it begins rolling.
* Contact between the cue stick and the cue ball must happen before the cue ball's forward motion.
* The cue ball must roll continuously toward its final position rather than teleporting there.
* The cue ball must slow down before coming to a complete stop.
* The cue ball must approach the group of balls before settling near them.
* The stopping event must occur after the rolling and deceleration stages.
* The cue ball must not disappear, duplicate, or change position discontinuously during the sequence.
* The nearby group of balls should not move unless the cue ball or another object physically contacts them.
