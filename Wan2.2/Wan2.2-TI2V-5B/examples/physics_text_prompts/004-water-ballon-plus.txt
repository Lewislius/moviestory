[Original prompt]
A water balloon is thrown against a brick wall, splattering water and leaving a wet mark.

Relevant physical states:

* The water balloon must remain present and visually consistent before impact.
* The brick wall must remain present, stationary, and spatially consistent throughout the sequence.
* The distance between the water balloon and the wall should decrease during the approach.
* The balloon should remain intact before contacting the wall.
* Physical contact between the balloon and the wall must occur at the impact moment.
* The balloon should deform and rupture as a consequence of the impact.
* Water should emerge from the ruptured balloon and spread outward from the impact region.
* The wall surface should become visibly wet after the water contacts it.
* A wet mark should remain on the wall after the splashing event.

Physical event order:

1. The intact water balloon moves toward the brick wall.
2. The distance between the balloon and the wall decreases.
3. The water balloon makes physical contact with the wall.
4. The impact causes the balloon to deform and rupture.
5. Water is released and splatters outward from the impact location.
6. The released water contacts the wall surface.
7. A visible wet mark forms and remains on the wall after the splash.

Cause-effect constraints:

* The water balloon and the brick wall must exist before the collision can occur.
* The balloon must approach the wall before making contact with it.
* Physical contact with the wall must happen before the balloon ruptures.
* The balloon rupture must be caused by the impact rather than occurring spontaneously before contact.
* Water release must happen after the balloon ruptures.
* Water splattering must originate from the impact and rupture region.
* The wet mark must form only after water contacts the wall.
* The balloon must not disappear, duplicate, or release water before the rupture event.
* The wet mark must not appear on the wall before the splash occurs.
